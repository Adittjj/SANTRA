Four things we do with your clothes

Repair first

38% of what arrives only needs a seam, a zip or a wash. Repair beats recycling, so it is always the first option.

Mechanical recycling

The rest is shredded and re-spun without chemical solvents. No virgin cotton since 2023.

Plastic-free returns

Bags, mailers and tags are paper or cassava starch. The bag your order arrives in comes back with your next donation.

Named planting

Every garment funds one native sapling, tagged to its donor and audited at 12, 24 and 36 months.

How we got here

2018 - Santra opens as a small clothing label in Pune, buying conventional cotton like everyone else.

2021 - First take-back bin at the Pune store. 1,900 garments come back in four months and the mill idea starts.

2023 - We stop buying virgin fibre entirely. Every new piece is made from donated material.

2024 - One tree per garment becomes a standing promise, with donor names on every sapling.

2026 - 1.28 million trees standing, 34 cities served, and doorstep pickup made free for everyone.

2030 - The target: five million named trees and a million garments recovered every year.
