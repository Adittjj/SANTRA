What you can send

Condition does not matter. Holes, stains and missing buttons all have a route through the mill.

Send it over

  • Shirts, kurtas, dresses, trousers, denim
  • Knitwear, coats, jackets, blazers
  • Saris, dupattas, bed linen, curtains, towels
  • Shoes and bags, even single shoes
  • Torn, stained, shrunk or faded anything

Please keep these back

  • Wet or mould-damaged fabric
  • Items with oil, paint or chemicals
  • Medical or PPE textiles
  • Foam-filled mattresses and pillows
  • Anything you might miss in a month

Where your bag goes

Day 0

Collected

Our rider weighs the bag at your door and you get an SMS receipt.

Day 2

Sorted by hand

Sorters in Pune split the bag by fibre, colour and condition. About 38% can be resold as it is.

Day 9

Repaired or shredded

Wearable pieces are mended. The rest is re-spun and woven into new cloth.

Day 30

Trees planted

Your saplings go in at a restoration site and the certificate lands in your account.

Questions we get asked

Do I really get a tree for every single garment?

Yes, counted per item and not per bag. A bag of nine shirts plants nine trees. Planting happens in batches at the start of each month.

Can I plant in someone else's name?

You can. Put their name in the certificate field and we'll email the certificate to them with a note saying who donated.

What happens to fibres that can't be recycled?

About 6% is blended synthetic our mill can't process. It goes to a cement kiln for energy recovery rather than landfill.

Is my credit transferable?

Credit stays in your account for 24 months and can be sent to any other account by email. It can be spent on anything in the catalogue, including repairs.
